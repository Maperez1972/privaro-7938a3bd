## Copy — long (~600 chars)
> Privaro is AI privacy governance infrastructure for regulated enterprises. It acts as a proxy layer between enterprise apps or AI agents and LLM providers: every prompt is scanned by a hybrid detection engine (regex + NLP), sensitive entities (PII, financial data, health data, contracts, identifiers) are tokenized, anonymized or blocked according to policy, and originals are stored in an encrypted AES-256 vault with controlled reveal. Every interaction is logged with optional blockchain certification, so legal, fintech, healthcare and compliance teams get a defensible audit trail out of the box.
